# breakaway licence exception agreement

This is the template the owner fills in and signs to grant an exception: a free commercial licence for one group and one use, as [`LICENSING.md`](../LICENSING.md#exceptions) describes. An exception is agreed first, on the group's [licence exception request](https://github.com/TheAnarchoX/breakaway/issues/new?template=licence-exception.yml) or by email, and only becomes a licence once both sides have signed a copy of this agreement. It's plain on purpose, and it isn't legal advice: if you're not sure what it means for your group, ask someone who can give you that.

The parts in square brackets are filled in for each exception. Everything else stays as it is.

## The agreement

This agreement is between:

- **The licensor**: the licensor named in breakaway's [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE) ("the owner").
- **The grantee**: [the group's legal name], [the kind of group: a worker co-op, or a digital rights or privacy group], at [the group's address or registration, and a link to its site or its work] ("the group").

It starts on [the date the second side signs] and was agreed on [a link to the request on GitHub, or "by email"].

1. **What it grants.** breakaway's licence, the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0, covers only the permitted purposes it names. The owner grants the group a licence to use, change, and self-host breakaway for the use below as well, as if it were a permitted purpose. Everything else in PolyForm Noncommercial still applies to the group, including its notices, its patent defence, and its "no liability" terms.
2. **The use.** [What the group uses breakaway for: the work it runs, for whom, and roughly how many people use it.] A use this point doesn't name needs its own exception.
3. **Limits.** [Any limits the owner set, like a number of installs or people, or "None beyond this agreement."]
4. **How long.** [Until a date, or "Until it ends under point 7."]
5. **Free.** The group pays nothing for this licence, now or later, and it never comes with a price, a plan, or a paid feature.
6. **Only for the group.** The licence is the group's alone. The group can't transfer it, sublicense it, or pass it on with breakaway, including to a group that buys it, merges with it, or takes over its work. Its members and staff may use breakaway for the group's use, as part of the group.
7. **When it ends.** The licence ends on its own, without notice, if the group stops fitting the [exception policy](../LICENSING.md#exceptions) it was granted under: if it takes money from venture capital, is bought by or becomes part of a company that does, stops being a worker co-op or a digital rights or privacy group, or uses breakaway beyond point 2 or 3. The group tells the owner as soon as one of these happens. Either side can also end it by writing to the other; it then ends [30] days later. Once it ends, the group stops using breakaway for anything PolyForm Noncommercial doesn't cover.
8. **Releases.** It covers every breakaway release from 2.0.0 on that the group uses while it's in force. Releases before 2.0.0 stay under FSL-1.1-Apache-2.0.
9. **As is.** The owner gives breakaway as it is, without any warranty or support, as PolyForm Noncommercial says.
10. **The whole agreement.** This is everything the owner and the group agreed about the exception. A change is a new copy, signed by both.

## How it's signed

The owner fills in the square brackets from what was agreed on the request or by email, and sends the group the filled-in copy. Someone who can sign for the group signs it and sends it back, and the owner signs it too. Each side keeps a signed copy. The owner never puts a signed copy, or anyone's details from it, in this repository or on a public request: on a request on GitHub, the owner only says that the exception is signed, and closes it.
