# breakaway's contributor licence agreement

> **Draft, waiting for legal review.** This agreement is plain on purpose and hasn't yet been reviewed by a lawyer. The owner's legal review (BRK-297) may change it before breakaway 2.0.0 ships, and a changed agreement is signed again.

This is an individual contributor licence agreement ("the agreement") between you and breakaway's owner, the licensor named in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) ("the owner"). It covers every contribution you send to breakaway's repository, now or later.

## Why it exists

breakaway is licensed under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 from 2.0.0 on, and its owner grants commercial exceptions case by case. To do that for the whole of breakaway, the owner needs the right to license your contribution on the same terms as the rest of it. You keep the copyright in what you wrote; this agreement gives the owner a licence to it.

## The agreement

1. **Contribution.** A "contribution" is any code, documentation, or other work you send to breakaway's repository (a pull request, a patch, a comment with code in it) for inclusion in breakaway. Something you mark plainly as "not a contribution" isn't one.
2. **Copyright licence.** You grant the owner, and anyone who receives breakaway from the owner, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable licence to use, copy, change, publish, distribute, sublicense, and relicense your contributions and works based on them, under any terms the owner chooses, including commercial terms and licences other than breakaway's licence at the time.
3. **Patent licence.** You grant the owner, and anyone who receives breakaway from the owner, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable licence under any patent you can license that your contribution, alone or combined with breakaway, would infringe, to make, use, sell, offer for sale, import, and otherwise transfer it. If anyone sues claiming breakaway or a contribution infringes a patent, the patent licences they received under this agreement end on the day they sue.
4. **Yours to give.** You confirm each contribution is your own original work, and that you have the right to grant these licences. If your employer, or anyone else, has rights in what you make, you confirm they allowed you to contribute it, or waived those rights, under this agreement.
5. **Someone else's work.** If you send work that isn't yours, you say so in the pull request, with where it came from and its licence, and mark it plainly. It is only included if its licence lets the owner license it as point 2 says.
6. **No promise to use it.** The owner may use your contribution, change it, or leave it out.
7. **As is.** You give your contributions as they are, without any warranty, unless you agree otherwise in writing.
8. **Telling the owner.** If anything you confirm here stops being true, you tell the owner as soon as you know, in a comment on the pull request where you signed.

## How you sign it

When you open a pull request, the CLA check asks you to sign by commenting on it, once, with exactly:

> I have read the breakaway CLA and I sign it.

Your GitHub username, your account's ID, and the pull request you signed on are kept in this repository, on the `cla-signatures` branch. One signature covers all your later contributions.
