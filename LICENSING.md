# Licensing

breakaway is free for personal and noncommercial use. From 2.0.0, its licence is the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). This page says what that means in plain words. It isn't legal advice: where this page and the licence differ, the licence decides.

## Who uses it free

The licence lets you use, change, and self-host breakaway, and share it with its licence, for a permitted purpose. It names two kinds:

- **Personal use**: research, experiment, and testing for the benefit of public knowledge, personal study, private entertainment, hobby projects, amateur pursuits, or religious observance, without any commercial application in view.
- **Noncommercial organizations**: charities, educational institutions, public research organizations, public safety or health organizations, environmental protection organizations, and government institutions, however they're funded.

## What counts as commercial

Any use the licence doesn't name as a permitted purpose. If your use isn't personal use as above, and you aren't one of the organizations it lists, the licence doesn't cover it, and you need an exception.

## Exceptions

The owner grants a free commercial licence case by case, to groups whose work fits what breakaway is for:

- **Worker co-ops.**
- **Digital rights and privacy groups**, including projects that build software to give people back ownership of their digital lives, after the owner has looked at the work.

A company funded by venture capital can't get an exception, whatever its size. Asking is no promise of a yes.

How to ask, and what to include, is on [leavethepack.dev/licensing](https://leavethepack.dev/licensing/).

## Releases before 2.0.0

Every release before 2.0.0 stays under the licence it shipped with, FSL-1.1-Apache-2.0, whose text is in [`LICENSE-FSL`](LICENSE-FSL). The PolyForm Noncommercial License applies to 2.0.0 and every release after it.
